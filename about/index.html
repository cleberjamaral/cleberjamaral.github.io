About

Background

I completed my technical studies in Electronics and Microprocessor Systems at ETFSC in 1999. I graduated in Electronic Systems at CEFET-SC in 2007, earned an MBA in Business Management from Univali in 2012, obtained a Master’s degree in Mechatronics from the Federal Institute of Santa Catarina (IFSC) in 2016, and received a PhD from UFSC in 2022 in the Graduate Program in Automation and Systems Engineering.

I worked for 15 years in the electronics industry, first as an entrepreneur and later at Automatiza and Intelbras. During this period, I worked on the development and management of hardware and software project teams. Since 2016, I have been a lecturer at the Federal Institute of Santa Catarina (IFSC), teaching digital electronics, programming languages, embedded devices, and telecommunications projects. I am currently on a sabbatical leave from IFSC, working as a full-stack developer at Artisan building Autonomous BDR agents.

My research interests are organisations and development tools for Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), Distributed Systems, Machine Learning, and Programming Languages.
