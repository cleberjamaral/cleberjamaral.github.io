Extension

Projects

  • Oficinas de Introdução à lógica de Programação (Parceria NAAHs) was a project of programming workshops for children (around 11 years old). It used Scratch as the programming platform and Raspberry Pi as the hardware platform. The project ran from 11/2016 to 11/2017 with an IFSC team coordinated by Cleber Jorge Amaral, with supervisors Adriano Régis, Francisco Édson de Melo, and Mauricio Edgar Stivanello, and student Felipe D'Avila Mesquita. From FCEE, the team included Sirlei Ignacio, Ana Paula, and others, with help from volunteer Isis and others.
  • Tutoria NAAH/S was a pilot project for tutoring of gifted students in project management and programming. A project was chosen and developed in three months (10/2016 to 12/2016), coordinated and supervised by Cleber Jorge Amaral with support from FCEE, especially Sirlei Ignacio and Ana Paula.
